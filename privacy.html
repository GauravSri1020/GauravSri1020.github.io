This policy explains how GSX Shorts Autopilot ("the app") handles data when you sign in with your Google account.

What data the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses Google OAuth to access your YouTube account so it can upload videos and manage videos on your own channel. It receives an access token and a refresh token from Google.

How the data is used

Tokens are used only to upload and manage videos on the channel that granted access. The app does not read, sell, share, or transfer your Google user data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private infrastructure and are never published.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for deletion of any stored data, email the contact below.
